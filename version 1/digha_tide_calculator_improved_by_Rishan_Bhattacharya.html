The Harmonic Model

η(t) = Z₀ + Σᵢ Hᵢ · cos(ωᵢt − gᵢ)
ConstituentOriginSpeed ω (°/hr)Amplitude H (m)Phase g (°)

Method: this version fits a frequency-constrained harmonic model directly to the high/low times and heights in the supplied Digha tide table. Amplitudes and phases are estimated by least squares; they are not manually entered. The model is then extrapolated to the selected date.

Important: the supplied source contains only tidal extrema, not continuous sea-level observations. Therefore this is an empirical harmonic extrapolation, not a certified astronomical tide prediction. A continuous 30+ day tide-gauge record would be needed for professional harmonic constants.

Approximation only — not for navigation. This model is fitted to reported high/low extrema rather than continuous tide-gauge observations. Real tides are also affected by wind, atmospheric pressure, river discharge and storm surge — none of which this model includes. For safety-critical use, consult Survey of India / INCOIS / Indian National Hydrographic Office tide tables.
